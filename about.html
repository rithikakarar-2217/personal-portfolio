BIOGRAPHY // PERSPECTIVE

Authentic Curiosity, Grounded Ambition

An honest look at who I am, how I learn, what keeps me inspired outside engineering, and where I hope my journey takes me.

01 // THE STARTING LINE

Hello, I'm Rithika

I am a first-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at BGS College of Engineering and Technology (graduating class of 2030).

Entering college, I made a conscious commitment to be honest about where I stand. I am not an industry veteran with dozens of repositories, but rather a curious, dedicated beginner who loves figuring out how things work under the hood. For my college Innovation and Design Thinking (IDT) activity, I wanted to create a portfolio that reflects this authentic perspective rather than mimicking a generic resume template.

02 // THE FASCINATION

Why Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning?

My interest in AI and Machine Learning doesn't stem from hype; it comes from a deep fascination with how simple mathematics and logic rules can combine to perceive patterns, understand human speech, and assist with complex decisions.

I am fascinated by how machines learn from examples rather than just executing rigid hard-coded instructions. As a first-year student, my focus is on mastering the building blocks — logic structures, algorithmic thinking, and clean code — so that as our college curriculum progresses into linear algebra, probability, and neural networks, I have the foundational discipline needed to thrive.

03 // LEARNING PHILOSOPHY

Step-by-Step, Without Shortcuts

Right now, I am actively learning C programming fundamentals, exploring C++ for object-oriented principles, picking up Python basics for data manipulation, and building responsive web interfaces with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

I follow a simple rule: when something breaks, I don't just copy a quick solution. I trace the variable values, check the terminal error output, or inspect the browser console. Building my XOXO Tic-Tac-Toe game proved to me that real learning happens during the process of debugging edge cases.

04 // FUTURE HORIZONS

Career Aspirations: Becoming an AI Engineer

My goal over my four years of undergraduate engineering is to evolve into a proficient AI Engineer. I want to build practical software and machine learning models that address meaningful problems — whether in healthcare diagnostics, education accessibility, or everyday utility tools.

To achieve this, my immediate roadmap is to solidify my problem-solving ability in C/C++, deepen my Python data analysis skills, and create collaborative projects with my college peers.
