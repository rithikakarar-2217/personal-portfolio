PHASE 01 // CURIOSITY

01 / Questions & Ideas

Every engineering journey begins with authentic curiosity. Rather than looking for complex pre-built frameworks, I ask foundational questions: How does a browser interpret code? How does a computer keep track of turns in a game? What problems can simple logic solve?

›Identifying everyday logic problems that can be solved with software.
›Framing ideas through the Innovation & Design Thinking (IDT) lens: User need first.
›Keeping an engineering journal for raw thoughts, diagrams, and questions.

“I used to think programming was about memorizing code. Now I see it's about breaking questions down until they become simple enough to solve.”
